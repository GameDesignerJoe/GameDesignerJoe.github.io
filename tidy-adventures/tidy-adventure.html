Tidy House

Everything's on the floor. Every item belongs in one specific piece of furniture — the fridge, the toolbox, the shoe rack. Toss things in, then open them up and sort the rows. A row of 5 matching items glows gold; finish every row in every container.

  • Tap an item — pick it up (5 hands max)
  • Swipe or tap a door — walk through it
  • Double-tap floor — zoom in / out (drag to look around)
  • Drag anything onto furniture — it files itself into the right row
  • Double-tap furniture — peek inside; drag items out to toss them on the floor
  • In an open container — tap a hand slot to file that item
  • Double-tap a hand slot — toss it back on the floor
  • Icons above furniture — what's inside; gold aura = that set is done
  • Locks — some doors and furniture need keys 🔑; drag keys onto the lock
  • ⭐ per completed row — spend them on upgrades (tap the star)
