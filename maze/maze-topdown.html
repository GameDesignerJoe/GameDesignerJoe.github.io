Maze

Find your way out.

Move with the stick at the bottom. Hold a direction and you'll glide; push another way and you'll take the next turn there.

Find the exit. It's a ring in one of the far corners. You start in the bottom-left.

Chalk is your memory. You start with 3. The counter at the top shows what's left. Loose pieces lie in dead ends — walk over one to take it.

Mark the floor by tapping your block (or the counter). One chalk, one X. Stand on an X and tap again to pick the chalk back up.
